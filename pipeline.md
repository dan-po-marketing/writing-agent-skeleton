# Writing Pipeline

## Step 0: Scope

Identify whether the input contains one idea or several.

If there are several, stop and ask the user to choose:

- split into separate pieces
- pick one and park the rest
- expand the strongest thin idea

## Step 1: Frame

Before drafting, answer:

- What is this piece trying to do?
- What is the core claim?
- What is the reader likely to misunderstand?
- What concrete proof or mechanism supports the claim?
- What should the reader remember after ten seconds?
- Which claims need modest wording?

## Step 2: Choose Shape

Pick the closest medium-specific archetype:

- Build story
- Tactical lesson
- Quick use-case
- Point-of-view

## Step 3: Draft

Write one clear draft using the chosen medium's style rules.

## Step 4: Critique

Score the draft from 1-5 on:

- Value
- Accuracy
- Voice
- Compression
- Specificity
- Medium fit

Revise only when a change preserves the core idea and fixes a real defect.
